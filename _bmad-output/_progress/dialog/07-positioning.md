## Positioning Statement

For someone who wants a trustworthy monthly picture of where their money goes but is unwilling to trade bank-linking or broad permissions for it, Tracker is a manual expense logger that fits exactly how you actually want to log spending. Unlike existing manual-entry apps (Monefy, Goodbudget, Pocket Clear) or a spreadsheet, it's built and owned by you — free to evolve on your own terms rather than someone else's roadmap.

**For:** Himself (audience of one)
**Who:** Has a vague sense of overspending but no concrete numbers, and rejects trackers demanding bank-linking/SMS/notification access
**This product:** A manual expense logger (web app, .NET Core + Angular)
**That:** Delivers exact fit to his own habits with zero setup friction
**Unlike:** Monefy, Goodbudget, Pocket Clear, spreadsheets, or automation-heavy apps
**Our approach:** Self-built and self-owned, so it can be shaped exactly to his workflow and evolved freely — not a claim of technical superiority

---

## Supporting Evidence

**Why this position makes sense:**
1. The PRFAQ already validated that manual-entry-only trackers are a real, if crowded, niche (Pocket Clear, Finny, Koody, Monefy, Goodbudget) — none dominant, but the approach is proven to attract a real audience.
2. Automation-heavy competitors are independently confirmed (2026 sources) to break trust via excessive permission requests — reinforcing that zero-setup-friction is a legitimate choice, not a corner cut.
3. Since v1 has no external users, "differentiation" in the classic competitive sense barely applies — ownership and exact fit are the only differentiators that matter at n=1, and they're genuine.
