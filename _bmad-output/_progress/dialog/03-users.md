## Primary User Definition

**Who they are:**
The founder himself — sole user, sole stakeholder, sole decision-maker. First-time solo product builder (per client profile).

**Their context:**
Spends mostly digitally (card/UPI). Transactions are technically reconstructable after the fact via bank/card statements, but he's choosing deliberate manual logging over automated capture — an ownership/control choice, not a technical necessity.

**Their frustrations:**
No consistent tracking today — just a vague, unconfirmed sense of overspending in certain categories. The realistic risk to sustained use isn't motivation or logging feeling like a chore; it's simply forgetting to log in the moment when a purchase happens.

**What they're trying to achieve:**
A trustworthy, periodic (roughly monthly) glance at spending broken down by category — enough to confirm or correct the vague sense of "I probably spend too much on X."

**How they currently solve this:**
Nothing structured — mental estimation only. Has deliberately ruled out automation-heavy trackers (bank-linking, SMS/notification access) due to trust/permission concerns, and ruled out existing manual-entry apps and spreadsheets in favor of building his own.
