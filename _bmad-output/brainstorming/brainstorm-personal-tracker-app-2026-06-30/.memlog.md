- (idea by user) Read phone SMS/messages to auto-detect and track expenses from bank/payment alerts
- (idea by user) Track UPI payments (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm) via push notifications
- (idea by user) Track online shopping expenses from Amazon, Flipkart etc. automatically

- (idea by user) Track money-in too — bill splits, repayments, cash received
- (idea by user) Monthly spending breakdown by category — visual dashboard
- (idea by user) AI suggestions to cut unnecessary expenses and improve savings
- (decision) App personality/tone: cheerleader — celebratory, encouraging, never shaming
- (idea by user) Cheer for financial discipline and good money habits, not just savings numbers
- (idea by user) Personal pain point: buying more quantities than needed — over-purchasing habit
- (idea by user) Pre-purchase checklist triggered before confirming big orders — friction before commitment
- (idea by user) Smart purchase value score — based on need, importance, and growth impact, not fixed rupee threshold
- (decision) Growth Spending categories: books, courses, gym, growth tools — always celebrate, never flag as overspend
- (idea by user) Gamification to beat abandonment: calendar ticking, badges, streaks — make tracking addictive
- (insight) Most emotionally meaningful badge: First ₹1000 Saved — the milestone that proves it works
- (idea by user) Analogies: personal finance diary (reflective, private, chronological) + piggy bank (saving-first, satisfying, tangible progress)
- (idea by user) Childhood money joy: collecting coins, earning rewards, star sticker charts — bring that feeling back
- (idea by user) The Financial Freedom Coin — rarest badge, earned after 6 months of consistent tracking
- (idea by user) Proactive coach: notice when about to overspend and intervene before it happens
- (decision) App voice: sounds like a real friend — warm, casual, personal — never robotic or corporate
- (idea by user) Monday morning motivational money tip — weekly kickoff message from the friend/coach
- (insight) 5-channel financial radar: SMS + UPI push + email + voice + cash widget — capture without thinking
- (insight) Spending philosophy engine: pre-purchase checklist + PVS + Growth Spending = one coherent system answering 'is this money doing something for me?'
- (insight) Friend voice = early prototype of the AI assistant — v1 personality IS the v-final product direction
- (insight) Progression arc: First ₹1000 → streaks → Financial Freedom Coin = Day 1 to Month 6 retention journey
