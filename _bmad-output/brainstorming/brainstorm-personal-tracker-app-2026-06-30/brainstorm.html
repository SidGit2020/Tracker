Technique 02 · Constraint Mapping

Every Wall
Has a Door

The real product obstacles surfaced here — the messy, honest ones. Each constraint became a design brief. The best features in FinFriend were born from refusing to accept these four walls.

🧱 The Wall

Cash is invisible money

₹200 for groceries at the sabzi mandi, ₹80 chai-sutta with the team — cash purchases evaporate. They're the biggest gap in any expense tracker.

↓
🚪 The Door

Home-screen quick-tap widget + ₹ button lock screen shortcut

Under 5 seconds from thought to logged. Cash becomes a first-class citizen, not an afterthought. The friction is lower than opening Instagram.

👤 SIDDI
🧱 The Wall

App abandonment kills every tracker

Week 1 is exciting. Week 3 the habit breaks. By month 2 the app is forgotten. Abandonment is the #1 cause of death for money apps.

↓
🚪 The Door

Gamification: streaks, calendar ticking, badges — make tracking addictive

Calendar fills up like a sticker chart. Streaks build momentum. Badges create emotional milestones. Each day logged makes the next day's log feel like protecting something precious.

👤 SIDDI
🧱 The Wall

The over-purchasing trap

Personal pain point from SIDDI: buying 3 when you need 1. Big basket syndrome. The purchase feels fine in the moment but the pattern costs real money over time.

↓
🚪 The Door

Pre-purchase checklist + Smart Purchase Value Score (PVS)

Friction before commitment on big orders. A PVS scored on need, importance, and growth impact — not a fixed rupee threshold. Growth spending (books, courses, gym) always scores high. Impulse bulk-buying scores low.

👤 SIDDI ✓ Decision: Growth categories always celebrated
🧱 The Wall

Financial shame makes people quit

Every other money app feels like a scolding parent. Red numbers, warning alerts, "you've overspent!" — shame doesn't change behaviour. It kills motivation and gets the app deleted.

↓
🚪 The Door

Cheerleader personality — never shame, always celebrate

Cheer for discipline and good habits, not just raw savings numbers. The AI notices when you're about to overspend and intervenes before it happens — like a friend who gently says "are you sure?" not a judge who punishes after.

✓ Core Decision: App Personality 👤 SIDDI

Session Decisions

What Got Decided

Six binding choices made during this session. These aren't ideas — they're commitments that shape every feature decision from here forward.

Decision 01

Mode: Creative Partner

Session set to maximum creative freedom — no idea too wild, no constraint sacred until earned. Theatrical and deep.

Decision 02

Scope: v1 Expense Tracker Only

Full AI assistant vision acknowledged and explicitly deferred. v1 is the expense tracker — done right, not done big.

Decision 03

Personality: The Cheerleader

Celebratory, encouraging, never shaming. This is the single most important design constraint in the whole product.

Decision 04

Growth Spending = Always Celebrate

Books, courses, gym, growth tools are permanently whitelisted. The app never flags these as overspending. Ever.

Decision 05

Voice: Real Friend, Not Robot

Warm, casual, personal in all copy and notifications. Sounds like a person who knows you and is rooting for you.

Decision 06

PVS = Need + Importance + Growth Impact

Smart Purchase Value Score scored on three dimensions — not a rupee threshold. Intelligent, not blunt.

✦ The Synthesis ✦

5 Connections That
Change Everything

Every brainstorm reaches a moment where the ideas stop being separate and start being a system. This is that moment.

1
🤖 Coach Insight · Capture System

The 5-Channel Financial Radar: capture without thinking

SMS + UPI push + email receipts + voice notes + cash widget isn't a feature list — it's a philosophy. The goal is that financial capture becomes truly frictionless. You shouldn't have to choose to track. It should just happen. Every channel plugs a different leak in the financial awareness bucket.

📨 SMS Auto-detect 📲 UPI Push · PhonePe · GPay · Paytm 📧 Email Receipts 🎙️ Voice Notes 💵 Cash Widget
2
🤖 Coach Insight · Decision System

The Spending Philosophy Engine: one coherent answer to "is this worth it?"

The pre-purchase checklist, the Smart Purchase Value Score, and Growth Spending whitelisting aren't three separate features. They're one system with one purpose: answering the question "is this money doing something for me?" before it's spent. The PVS gives the score. The checklist gives the pause. Growth spending gives the permission to spend freely on self-investment.

Pre-purchase checklist → pause before commitment
PVS (Need × Importance × Growth) → score the decision
Growth Spending whitelist → buy books/courses without guilt
3
🤖 Coach Insight · Product Direction

Friend voice = early prototype of the AI assistant — v1 personality IS the final product direction

The warm, casual, personal voice built for v1 isn't just an aesthetic. It's the skeleton of a full AI financial companion. How FinFriend talks in v1 — Monday morning tips, gentle pre-purchase check-ins, streak celebrations — is the first version of something that will eventually be the whole product. Get the voice right in v1, and you're already building in the right direction. Get it wrong, and v-final has to rebuild from the ground up.

"The v1 personality IS the v-final product direction. The AI assistant is already in the room."
4
🤖 Coach Insight · Retention Journey

The progression arc: First ₹1000 → streaks → Financial Freedom Coin = Day 1 to Month 6 retention

App abandonment is the real problem. The three-stage progression arc is the solution — structured to create emotional milestones at the precise moments when users are most at risk of quitting. First ₹1000 Saved converts the sceptic at Week 3–4. Streaks build the daily habit. The Financial Freedom Coin at 6 months is the identity shift — by then, being a "FinFriend user" is just who you are.

→
Month 1 → ₹1000 Badge 🥇

5
🤖 Coach Insight · Emotional Core

The First ₹1000 Saved is the most emotionally meaningful badge — the milestone that proves it works

Of all the badges in the system, this one carries the most weight. Not because ₹1000 is a large sum — but because it's the moment the product proves itself to the user. Before this badge: "this is a nice app." After this badge: "this app is working for me." That emotional shift from sceptic to believer is the most valuable transition in the entire product. Design every feature in v1 with this moment in mind. The First ₹1000 is not a milestone to celebrate — it's a product promise to keep.

"Design v1 backwards from the First ₹1000 moment. Every feature either makes that moment possible sooner, or it doesn't belong in v1."

One App. One Philosophy. Infinite Cheers.

FinFriend isn't an expense tracker. It's an identity shift — from "I'm bad with money" to "I am someone who is in control of my money." The five connections above are the blueprint. Build the capture system, build the spending philosophy engine, get the voice right, design the progression arc around the First ₹1000, and let the Financial Freedom Coin be the goal that makes every logged expense feel like a step toward something real.
