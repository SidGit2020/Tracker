# 02: Siddi Reviews the Month

**Project:** Tracker
**Created:** 2026-07-02
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Transaction (Q1)

**What this scenario covers:**
Review this month's spending by category and confirm the numbers are trustworthy — turn a vague feeling into a specific fact.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** Trust the Number (Prerequisite)
**Objective:** Objective 1 — Exact, Immediate Reflection; Objective 3 — Spot-Check Accuracy

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Siddi the Self-Tracker (Primary — only persona)
**Situation:** It's near month-end, he's unhurried — not mid-task — sitting down specifically to check in on where his money went.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** The category totals finally give him a specific number instead of a vague feeling of overspending.

**Worry:** A total looks off and he can't tell if it's a bug or he really spent that much.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Desktop or mobile — equal priority; this is a reflective session, not urgent.
**Entry:** Opens the bookmarked app during an unhurried moment near month-end, specifically to check spending rather than to log anything new.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:**
Confirms exactly how much he spent in each category this month, replacing the nagging feeling with a specific trustworthy number.

**Business Success:**
Validates the "trust the number" prerequisite (zero discrepancy, spot-check confirmed) that keeps the day-14+ habit alive.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Monthly Category Breakdown** — Opens the breakdown and sees this month's per-category totals at a glance
2. **Monthly Category Breakdown** — Spot-checks one or two categories against his own memory of what he spent ✓

---

## Trigger Map Connections

**Persona:** Siddi the Self-Tracker (Primary)

**Driving Forces Addressed:**
- ✅ **Want:** Get a specific, trustworthy number that replaces the vague feeling
- ❌ **Fear:** Distrusting a number that looks wrong

**Business Goal:** Trust the Number — exact/immediate reflection, spot-check accuracy
