Tracker v1 Scope

"Tracker v1 = manual expense entry only, kept simple" — pressure-tested against locked prior strategy

Verdict

Hardened. The idea survived contact with two days of prior locked strategy work. What started as a vague "keep it simple" was forced apart, tested against a real payoff question, and re-assembled into a scope that is deliberately narrower than — but not in conflict with — the existing innovation strategy.

What Locked

  • Manual expense entry only — no SMS/UPI/email/voice capture
  • Fixed, short, predefined category list — no category management UI
  • Recurring bills (rent/EMI/tuition) logged as normal entries — no recurrence engine
  • Monthly total per category — plain sums, no charts or trends
  • No friend-voice, no tone, no gamification — v1 has an audience of one
  • No investment/retirement advice
  • This precedes the innovation strategy's Horizon 1 / Phase 1 rather than contradicting it — those begin once v-next brings in other users
  • Platform confirmed: web app, .NET Core + Angular (carried over from 2026-07-01 design thinking)

What Was Killed

  • Automated capture (SMS/UPI/email/voice) — deferred, not this version
  • Category add/edit management
  • Gamification: streaks, badges
  • Friend/coach tone and personality
  • Investment and retirement advice
  • A no-summary, pure-log version — considered and rejected: zero payoff for the effort of logging

Cracks That Held

  • "Keep it simple" was hiding two unrelated cuts — capture channels vs. the tone/moat layer — collapsed into one vague phrase until forced apart
  • The literal "bare minimum" reading (logging with no summary at all) would have shipped a tool that gives nothing back — caught before it locked

Why This Scope Holds

v1 has no external users, so the trust/tone layer the innovation strategy calls the real moat has no one to build trust with yet — cutting it isn't losing the moat, it's correctly sequencing it for later. Cutting the tone was tested twice: once nearly kept because it costs almost nothing to build, then re-confirmed cut anyway once reframed as an audience decision rather than a cost decision. Recurring bills need no special engineering — they're expenses like any other. And a log with no summary was rejected outright: it wouldn't have survived past week two, even for an audience of one.
