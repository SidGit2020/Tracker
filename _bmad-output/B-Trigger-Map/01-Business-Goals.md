# Business Goals & Objectives

> Strategic goals and measurable objectives for Tracker

**Document:** Trigger Map - Business Goals
**Created:** 2026-07-02
**Status:** COMPLETE

---

## Vision

**Turn a vague sense of overspending into a trustworthy, always-current number — built solo, owned completely, and simple enough that logging never feels like a chore.**

Tracker has no growth or revenue ambitions. Its entire strategic purpose is behavioral: prove that removing setup and logging friction is enough to keep one person tracking past the point where every prior attempt (mental estimation, other apps) quietly stopped.

---

## Business Objectives

### 🎯 PRIMARY GOAL: Build a Lasting Personal Logging Habit (THE BEHAVIORAL BET)
- **Statement:** Siddi keeps logging expenses long enough for the habit to stick, not just for the first excited week.
- **Metric:** Active logging at day 14 post-launch
- **Target:** Still logging, with no missed-week gaps in month 1, and entries taking only a few seconds
- **Timeline:** Measured continuously from launch
- **Impact:** This is what the launch is actually meant to test — everything else exists to make this possible

---

### 📊 PREREQUISITE: Trust the Number

**Objective 1: Exact, Immediate Reflection**
- **Statement:** Every logged amount appears in the running total immediately and exactly
- **Metric:** Running total accuracy
- **Target:** Zero discrepancy between logged entries and displayed totals
- **Timeline:** True from day 1 of launch

**Objective 2: No Judgmental Copy**
- **Statement:** No UI text ever moralizes about spending amounts
- **Metric:** Presence/absence of judgmental language in microcopy
- **Target:** Zero instances, verified against the Tone of Voice guidelines
- **Timeline:** True from day 1 of launch

**Objective 3: Spot-Check Accuracy**
- **Statement:** Category totals match a manual spot-check
- **Metric:** Manual verification against logged entries
- **Target:** Confirmed accurate at each monthly review
- **Timeline:** Ongoing, checked monthly

---

### 🚀 PREREQUISITE: Ship v1 Within the Self-Imposed Window (Driven by Solo Discipline)

**Objective 1: Weekend-Scale Build**
- **Statement:** v1 ships within the self-imposed "days" build target
- **Metric:** Calendar time from start to launch
- **Target:** Days, not weeks or months
- **Timeline:** Immediate — this is the build window itself

**Objective 2: Locked Scope**
- **Statement:** The v1 scope locked in the forged-idea document holds through the build
- **Metric:** Scope changes during build
- **Target:** Zero scope creep; feature requests deferred to future iterations
- **Timeline:** Throughout the build

**Objective 3: Core Features Live at Launch**
- **Statement:** The quick-add box and live running total both work on day 1
- **Metric:** Feature completeness at launch
- **Target:** Both present and functional at ship time
- **Timeline:** At launch

---

## How the Goals Connect

**THE BEHAVIORAL BET (Priority #1):**
- Build a lasting personal logging habit
- This is the only goal with an external, falsifiable test (day-14 usage)
- Everything else exists to make this achievable

**Trust the Number (Priority #2):**
- Enables the habit goal — an untrusted number gets abandoned as fast as a slow one
- Exact, immediate, non-judgmental data is the mechanism

**Ship Within the Window (Priority #3):**
- Gates whether the other two goals are ever tested at all
- No external deadline enforces this — only Siddi's own discipline

---

## Success Metrics Alignment

**🎯 PRIMARY: Build a Lasting Logging Habit → Achieves:**
- ✅ Active logging at day 14 (the actual product-market fit signal, at n=1)
- ✅ No missed-week gaps in month 1
- ✅ Sub-few-second entry time
- **This is what the launch is designed to test**

**📊 Trust the Number → Achieves:**
- ✅ Exact, immediate totals (no estimates, no delay)
- ✅ Zero judgmental copy (protects against guilt-driven abandonment)
- ✅ Monthly spot-check accuracy confirmed

**🚀 Ship Within the Window → Achieves:**
- ✅ Weekend-scale build target met
- ✅ Locked scope held
- ✅ Core features live at launch

**The Trigger Map IS the strategic foundation** — every design decision should trace back to keeping logging fast and trustworthy enough that the habit survives past day 14.
